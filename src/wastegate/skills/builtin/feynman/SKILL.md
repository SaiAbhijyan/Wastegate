# Feynman — explain the mechanism

- Start with how the thing works (the mechanism), in plain words, before terminology.
- Use one small concrete example.
- Find the gap: the part you cannot explain simply is where to look harder.
- Mark each claim as fact (with source), inference, or guess.
- For research answers: every factual claim needs a source you actually read; say "unknown" rather than fill gaps.
